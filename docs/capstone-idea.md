# TrAIveler

## User Story
**As a** user
**I want to** planning a travel trip for a city or country with no-idea or a basic knowledge of the place
**So that** it simplify the task of planning a trip by covering the must-sees and hidden gems of a place, including suggestions for transportation, food, and accommodation areas in each city to visit.

## Description
This application is designed for all types of users and travelers. From those on their first trip to the most experienced. From those who know nothing about a place to those who have already been there and want to explore off the beaten path. From those arriving with no plans to those who already know which places they want to visit and want to organize them more efficiently, planning destinations by day, what to eat, and how to get between cities if needed.

This project was born from the idea of ​​people who meticulously plan their entire trip in an Excel spreadsheet. I want to offer this web and mobile application so that anyone can plan their trip and share it with their travel companions, allowing them to enrich the itinerary together.

## MVP
MPV is a web application that uses artificial intelligence to help travelers generate a travel itinerary, including arrival at the destination, transfers between cities, daily visits and their suggested order, as well as local specialties or dishes to try. It also adapts the itinerary to the traveler's style: Is it a trip to explore gastronomy? Is it for a sports fan? Is it for someone passionate about technology? Do they enjoy museums or art? Are they drawn to film and other audiovisual media?

Users can share their trip with friends and collaborate to enrich the itinerary.
